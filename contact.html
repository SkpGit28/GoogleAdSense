Contact

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This is a one-person site, so email is the only way to reach me. There is no phone line and no contact form. I read everything, though I do not always manage to reply quickly.

Corrections and hotel updates

contact@primehotelpicks.com

Use this for anything factual: a rate that has moved, a restaurant that has closed, a property under renovation, a hotel that has changed hands. If you have stayed somewhere I have written about and the guide does not match what you found, that is the most useful email I can get. Tell me roughly when you stayed and I will check and update the page.

Corrections get looked at first. I aim to reply within a week.

Hotels and PR

business@primehotelpicks.com

If you work for a property covered here and something is inaccurate, write in and I will fix it. Send the specific detail and the page it is on.

Two things worth saving us both the time on. I do not publish sponsored write-ups, and paying does not get a hotel added to a guide or moved up one. I also do not remove criticism in exchange for access, a media rate or a complimentary stay. If a criticism on this site is factually wrong, that is a correction and I will make it for free.

Advertising

business@primehotelpicks.com

Display advertising here is served through an ad network rather than sold directly, so there is no rate card. Advertisers have no influence over editorial content.

What I cannot help with

  • Bookings. This site is not a travel agency and cannot make, change or cancel a reservation. Contact the hotel or the platform you booked through.
  • Disputes with a hotel. Refunds, billing and complaints are between you and the property.
  • Personalised itineraries. I do not plan individual trips.
  • Guest posts and link requests. The answer is no, so please do not send the follow-up email either.

Privacy

If you email me, I keep the message so I can reply and so there is a record of the correction. I do not add anyone to a mailing list and I do not pass email addresses on. The privacy policy has the detail.
