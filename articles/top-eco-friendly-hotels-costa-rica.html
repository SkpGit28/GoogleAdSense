Top Eco-Friendly Hotels in Costa Rica

Jun 18, 2026 By Mr. Lee 9 min read
Advertisement

Costa Rica is a global leader in sustainable tourism, with hotels that combine luxury accommodations with deep environmental responsibility. From rainforest lodges to beachfront resorts, these eco-friendly properties prove that sustainability and luxury can go hand in hand.

In this guide, we've curated the top eco-friendly hotels in Costa Rica, each demonstrating exceptional commitment to environmental conservation while providing world-class guest experiences.

What Makes a Hotel "Eco-Friendly"?

True eco-friendly hotels demonstrate:

  • Renewable energy: Solar, wind, or hydroelectric power
  • Water conservation: Rainwater harvesting and greywater recycling
  • Waste reduction: Composting, recycling, and minimal plastic use
  • Local sourcing: Food and materials from local communities
  • Conservation efforts: Wildlife protection and habitat restoration

Top Eco-Friendly Hotels

1. Lapa Rios Lodge

Set on a 1,000-acre private nature reserve on the Osa Peninsula, Lapa Rios is one of Costa Rica's original eco-lodges. The property protects primary rainforest while offering luxury treehouse-style accommodations.

Why We Love It

  • 1,000-acre private nature reserve
  • Primary rainforest protection
  • Luxury treehouse-style bungalows
  • Carbon-neutral operations
  • Community education programs

2. Pacuare Lodge

Accessible only by river raft, Pacuare Lodge offers an unforgettable jungle experience. The lodge operates on hydroelectric power and supports local indigenous communities.

Why We Love It

  • Remote jungle location accessible by raft
  • Hydroelectric-powered operations
  • Supports local Cabécar community
  • Luxury suites with river views
  • Adventure activities included

3. Origins Lodge

This boutique eco-lodge in the Northern Highlands offers stunning views of Lake Arenal and the volcano. The property uses geothermal energy and practices regenerative agriculture.

4. Kura Design Villas

An adults-only boutique hotel in Uvita, Kura combines contemporary design with sustainability. The property features solar power, rainwater harvesting, and supports local conservation efforts.

5. Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa

Located near Arenal Volcano, Tabacón uses naturally heated thermal springs for heating and spa treatments. The resort's sustainability program includes waste reduction and local sourcing.

Sustainable Travel Tips for Costa Rica

  • Choose certified properties: Look for CST (Certification for Sustainable Tourism)
  • Support local communities: Eat at local restaurants and buy local products
  • Minimize plastic: Bring a reusable water bottle
  • Respect wildlife: Keep distance and never feed animals
  • Offset your carbon: Consider carbon offset programs for flights

Conclusion

Costa Rica's eco-friendly hotels demonstrate that luxury and sustainability are not mutually exclusive. These properties provide exceptional experiences while protecting the natural environment that makes Costa Rica so special.
