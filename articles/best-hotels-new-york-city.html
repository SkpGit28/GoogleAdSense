Best Hotels in New York City with a View

Jul 10, 2026 By Mr. Lee 8 min read
Advertisement

New York City is one of the most iconic cities in the world, and there's no better way to experience it than from a hotel with a stunning view. Whether you're gazing at the Manhattan skyline from a rooftop bar or watching Central Park change colors through your window, the right hotel view can transform your NYC experience.

In this guide, we've curated the best hotels in New York City that offer exceptional views. From luxury penthouses overlooking Times Square to boutique hotels with Central Park vistas, these accommodations will give you a front-row seat to the city that never sleeps.

What Makes a Great NYC Hotel View?

The best hotel views in New York City typically feature:

  • Manhattan skyline: The iconic skyscrapers that define NYC
  • Central Park: A green oasis in the heart of the city
  • Times Square: The dazzling lights and energy of Broadway
  • Hudson River: Stunning sunset views and the Statue of Liberty
  • Brooklyn Bridge: Historic architecture and waterfront views

Top Hotels with NYC Views

1. The Peninsula New York

Located on Fifth Avenue, The Peninsula offers some of the most stunning views in Midtown Manhattan. The hotel's rooftop bar, Clement, provides panoramic views of the city skyline, while many rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows with breathtaking vistas.

View Highlights

  • Central Park and Fifth Avenue views
  • Rooftop bar with 360-degree city views
  • Floor-to-ceiling windows in many rooms
  • Walking distance to Times Square and Broadway

2. Mandarin Oriental, New York

Perched atop the Time Warner Center, the Mandarin Oriental offers unparalleled views of Central Park and the Hudson River. The hotel's rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows that showcase the park's natural beauty in every season.

View Highlights

  • Direct Central Park views from most rooms
  • Hudson River and New Jersey skyline
  • Located at Columbus Circle
  • Access to Time Warner Center shopping and dining

3. The Standard, High Line

This trendy hotel in the Meatpacking District offers unique views of the High Line, Hudson River, and downtown Manhattan. The hotel's rooftop bar, Le Bain, features a plunge pool and stunning sunset views.

View Highlights

  • Hudson River and New Jersey views
  • High Line and Meatpacking District panorama
  • Rooftop bar with plunge pool
  • Walking distance to Chelsea Market

4. Park Hyatt New York

Occupying the top floors of a Midtown skyscraper, Park Hyatt New York offers sophisticated luxury with stunning city views. The hotel's rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows that showcase the Manhattan skyline.

5. The St. Regis New York

This historic Fifth Avenue hotel offers classic New York luxury with views of the Manhattan skyline and St. Patrick's Cathedral. The hotel's rooftop bar provides a stunning perspective on the city.

Budget-Friendly Hotels with Great Views

You don't need to break the bank for a great NYC view. Consider these options:

  • Pod 51: Affordable rooftop bar with Midtown views
  • The Jane Hotel: Historic hotel in West Village with Hudson River views
  • citizenM New York Bowery: Modern hotel with Manhattan skyline views

Tips for Booking a Room with a View

Follow these tips to ensure you get the best view:

  • Request a high floor when booking
  • Ask for a corner room for multiple view angles
  • Check if the view is "partial" or "full" before booking
  • Read recent reviews mentioning views
  • Consider booking directly with the hotel for upgrade opportunities

Conclusion

New York City offers some of the most spectacular hotel views in the world. Whether you choose a luxury penthouse or a boutique hotel with a rooftop bar, the right view can make your NYC trip truly unforgettable. These hotels combine exceptional views with comfortable accommodations and prime locations.
