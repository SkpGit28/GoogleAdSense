Top 10 Luxury Hotels in Bali

Jul 18, 2026 By Mr. Lee 10 min read
Advertisement

Bali, known as the Island of the Gods, is a tropical paradise that attracts millions of visitors each year. From its lush rice terraces and ancient temples to its stunning beaches and vibrant nightlife, Bali offers something for everyone. For travelers seeking the ultimate in luxury, the island boasts some of the finest hotels and resorts in Southeast Asia.

In this guide, we've curated the top 10 luxury hotels in Bali, each offering world-class amenities, exceptional service, and breathtaking settings. Whether you're looking for a private villa with an infinity pool overlooking the ocean or a secluded retreat nestled in the jungle, these hotels will exceed your expectations.

What Makes a Hotel "Luxury" in Bali?

Luxury in Bali is defined by a combination of factors: stunning natural settings, impeccable service, private accommodations, world-class dining, and unique cultural experiences. The best luxury hotels on the island understand that true luxury is about creating unforgettable memories and providing personalized attention to every guest.

Top 10 Luxury Hotels

1. Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan

Nestled in the lush Ayung River valley, the Four Seasons Sayan is a masterpiece of design and nature. The resort features stunning treehouse-like villas, a world-renowned spa, and exceptional dining experiences. Guests can enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding rainforest and rice paddies.

Why We Love It

  • Location: Ubud, surrounded by tropical rainforest
  • Price range: $500-1200 per night
  • Private villas with plunge pools
  • Award-winning spa and wellness center
  • Cultural immersion programs

2. Aman Villas at Nusa Dua

Aman Villas offers an unparalleled luxury experience with its spacious private villas, each featuring its own pool, garden, and dedicated butler service. The resort's minimalist design creates a sense of tranquility, while its beachfront location provides easy access to pristine white sand beaches.

Why We Love It

  • Location: Nusa Dua beachfront
  • Price range: $800-2000 per night
  • Private villas with personal butler
  • Direct beach access
  • Exceptional privacy and exclusivity

3. COMO Uma Ubud

COMO Uma Ubud is a boutique luxury resort that perfectly blends contemporary design with Balinese traditions. The resort offers stunning valley views, a world-class COMO Shambhala Retreat spa, and farm-to-table dining experiences that showcase the best of Indonesian cuisine.

Why We Love It

  • Location: Ubud, overlooking the Tjampuhan Valley
  • Price range: $300-600 per night
  • Wellness-focused experiences
  • Stunning valley views from every room
  • Authentic Balinese cultural experiences

4. The Mulia, Mulia Resort & Villas

This expansive resort complex in Nusa Dua offers multiple accommodation options, from elegant hotel rooms to luxurious private villas. The Mulia is known for its world-class spa, stunning beachfront location, and exceptional dining venues.

5. Viceroy Bali

Perched on a ridge overlooking the Petanu River valley, Viceroy Bali offers breathtaking views and intimate luxury. The resort's boutique approach ensures personalized service, while its infinity pool and restaurant provide some of the most spectacular views in Ubud.

6. Bulgari Resort Bali

Bulgari Resort Bali combines Italian luxury with Balinese traditions. Located on a clifftop in Uluwatu, the resort offers stunning ocean views, private villas with infinity pools, and access to a pristine private beach.

7. The St. Regis Bali Resort

Offering opulent suites and villas, The St. Regis Bali is synonymous with luxury. The resort features butler service, a stunning beachfront location, and some of the finest dining experiences in Bali.

8. Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve

Mandapa offers an intimate luxury experience in the heart of Ubud. With only 60 suites and villas, the resort provides an exclusive retreat surrounded by lush rice terraces and tropical forest.

9. Soori Bali

Soori Bali is a contemporary luxury resort located on the west coast, offering a unique blend of modern design and natural beauty. The resort's minimalist architecture creates a sense of space and tranquility.

10. Alila Villas Uluwatu

Perched on a limestone cliff, Alila Villas Uluwatu offers stunning ocean views and eco-luxury accommodations. The resort is known for its sustainable practices and innovative design.

Best Time to Visit Bali for Luxury Travel

The best time to visit Bali for luxury travel is during the dry season (April to October), when you can enjoy sunny days and comfortable temperatures. The peak season (July-August) offers the best weather but also the highest prices. For a balance of good weather and lower rates, consider visiting in April-May or September-October.

Conclusion

Bali's luxury hotels offer an extraordinary combination of natural beauty, world-class amenities, and exceptional service. Whether you choose a private villa in Ubud's rice terraces or a beachfront resort in Nusa Dua, you're guaranteed an unforgettable experience. These top 10 hotels represent the finest in Balinese hospitality and luxury.
