Best Hotels in Rome Near the Colosseum

Jun 25, 2026 By Mr. Lee 7 min read
Advertisement

The Colosseum is Rome's most iconic landmark, and staying nearby puts you at the heart of ancient history. The area surrounding the Colosseum offers a unique blend of archaeological wonders, charming cobblestone streets, and excellent dining options.

In this guide, we've curated the best hotels near the Colosseum, ranging from budget-friendly options to luxury properties. Each recommendation is based on proximity to the Colosseum, quality of accommodation, and overall value.

Why Stay Near the Colosseum?

The Colosseum area offers several advantages for visitors:

  • Walking distance to major attractions: Colosseum, Roman Forum, Palatine Hill
  • Authentic Roman atmosphere: Less touristy than the Vatican area
  • Excellent public transport: Metro line B connects to the entire city
  • Great dining: Local trattorias and restaurants away from tourist traps
  • Evening ambiance: The Colosseum is beautifully lit at night

Top Hotels Near the Colosseum

1. Hotel Palazzo Manfredi

This boutique hotel occupies a 17th-century palazzo with direct views of the Colosseum. The rooftop bar offers unparalleled sunset views of the ancient amphitheater, making it one of Rome's most romantic settings.

Why We Love It

  • Direct views of the Colosseum from many rooms
  • Rooftop bar with Colosseum views
  • 17th-century palazzo with modern comforts
  • Walking distance to Roman Forum
  • Boutique service and attention to detail

2. Forum Hotel

Located directly across from the Roman Forum, Forum Hotel offers excellent value with stunning views of ancient Rome. The hotel's central location makes it perfect for exploring the city's archaeological treasures.

Why We Love It

  • Direct views of the Roman Forum
  • Walking distance to Colosseum and Palatine Hill
  • Excellent value for the location
  • Rooftop terrace with panoramic views
  • Helpful staff with local knowledge

3. Hotel Lancelot

This charming family-run hotel is located just steps from the Colosseum. The hotel's warm Italian hospitality and comfortable rooms make it a favorite among repeat visitors to Rome.

4. Collateral Boutique Hotel

A modern boutique hotel in a historic building, Collateral offers stylish rooms with contemporary design. The hotel's location between the Colosseum and Circus Maximus makes it ideal for exploring ancient Rome.

5. The Nine Hotel

This design-forward hotel in the heart of the Colosseum district offers sophisticated accommodations with Italian flair. The hotel's restaurant serves excellent Roman cuisine, and its location provides easy access to all major attractions.

Budget-Friendly Options

Staying near the Colosseum doesn't have to break the bank. Consider these affordable options:

  • Hotel Celio: Simple, clean rooms at excellent prices
  • Romanico Palace: Good value with a rooftop restaurant
  • Albergo del Senato: Affordable with Forum views

Tips for Staying Near the Colosseum

  • Book early for Colosseum views: Rooms with views sell out quickly
  • Visit early morning: The Colosseum is less crowded before 9 AM
  • Buy skip-the-line tickets: Avoid long queues with advance booking
  • Explore on foot: The area is best explored by walking
  • Try local trattorias: Avoid touristy restaurants near the Colosseum

Conclusion

Staying near the Colosseum provides an unforgettable Roman experience. These hotels offer the perfect combination of location, comfort, and authentic Italian hospitality, ensuring your Roman holiday is truly memorable.
