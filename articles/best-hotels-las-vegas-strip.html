Best Hotels in Las Vegas on the Strip

Jun 15, 2026 By Mr. Lee 8 min read
Advertisement

The Las Vegas Strip is one of the most famous stretches of road in the world, lined with iconic resorts, casinos, and entertainment venues. Choosing the right hotel on the Strip can make or break your Vegas experience.

In this guide, we've curated the best hotels on the Las Vegas Strip, from ultra-luxury resorts to budget-friendly options. Each recommendation is based on location, amenities, value, and overall guest experience.

What to Consider When Choosing a Strip Hotel

  • Location on the Strip: Central location means easier access to attractions
  • Resort fees: Many Vegas hotels charge additional daily resort fees
  • Casino vs. non-casino: Some travelers prefer quieter, non-casino hotels
  • Dining options: Celebrity chef restaurants are a Vegas specialty
  • Entertainment: Shows, clubs, and attractions on-site
Advertisement

Top Hotels on the Strip

1. Bellagio

One of the most iconic hotels in Las Vegas, Bellagio is famous for its dancing fountains, fine dining, and elegant rooms. The hotel's central Strip location makes it perfect for exploring the city.

Why We Love It

  • Iconic dancing fountains
  • Central Strip location
  • Multiple celebrity chef restaurants
  • World-class casino and spa
  • Elegant, well-appointed rooms

2. The Venetian Resort

Inspired by Venice, The Venetian offers all-suite accommodations, a Grand Canal with gondola rides, and over 40 restaurants. The resort's size and amenities make it a destination within itself.

Why We Love It

  • All-suite accommodations
  • Grand Canal with gondola rides
  • 40+ restaurants and bars
  • Massive pool deck
  • Connected to The Palazzo

3. Wynn Las Vegas

Steve Wynn's masterpiece offers sophisticated luxury with stunning design, a championship golf course, and some of the finest dining in Vegas. The Wynn is known for exceptional service and attention to detail.

4. ARIA Resort & Casino

A modern, tech-forward resort, ARIA features keyless room entry, touchscreen controls, and floor-to-ceiling windows. The hotel's contemporary design appeals to younger travelers.

5. MGM Grand

One of the largest hotels in the world, MGM Grand offers something for everyone, from a massive casino to multiple pools and entertainment venues. Its central location makes it a popular choice.

Budget-Friendly Strip Hotels

  • The LINQ Hotel: Modern, affordable rooms with a central location
  • Flamingo: Classic Vegas charm at reasonable prices
  • Bally's: Good value with a central Strip location

Tips for Visiting Las Vegas

  • Book midweek: Rates are significantly lower Monday-Thursday
  • Check for resort fees: Budget an extra $30-50 per night
  • Get a players card: Earn points for free meals and shows
  • Walk the Strip: It's the best way to see the sights
  • Stay hydrated: The desert climate is deceptively dry

Conclusion

The Las Vegas Strip offers an incredible range of hotels to suit every budget and preference. Whether you choose a luxury resort or a budget-friendly option, you're guaranteed a memorable Vegas experience.
