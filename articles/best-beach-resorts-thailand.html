Thailand offers beach resort luxury at a fraction of Maldives or Caribbean prices. During my two weeks in March 2026, I visited five beach resorts across Phuket, Koh Samui, and Koh Kood, evaluating beach quality, snorkeling, privacy, and whether the lower price point means lower quality. Here's what I found — Thailand's best beaches genuinely rival the Indian Ocean, at 40-60% of the cost.

Top Beach Resorts

1. Soneva Kiri (Koh Kood)

Verified Stay

Soneva Kiri sits on Koh Kood, one of Thailand's least-developed islands. I stayed in a Beach Villa (300 sqm) with a private pool and direct beach access. The beach is stunning — powder-fine sand, turquoise water, and zero crowds. I saw exactly three other guests on the beach during my entire stay. The tree pod dining experience (a bamboo pod suspended in a tree canopy) was the most unique meal I've had in Thailand.

At $1,200/night all-inclusive, it's expensive for Thailand but half the price of Soneva Fushi in the Maldives with comparable quality. The all-inclusive covers all meals, premium drinks, and non-motorized water sports. The snorkeling directly from the beach was excellent — I saw parrotfish, clownfish, and a reef shark within 10 minutes. The remoteness requires a 1-hour flight from Bangkok plus a 45-minute speedboat, but that's what keeps it uncrowded.

Key Details

  • Location: Koh Kood, 1-hour flight + 45-min speedboat from Bangkok
  • Price: $800-3,000 per night (all-inclusive)
  • Villa: Beach Villa, 300 sqm, private pool, direct beach
  • Beach: Powder-fine sand, turquoise water, uncrowded
  • Snorkeling: Excellent — reef sharks visible from shore
  • vs. Maldives: 50% cheaper with comparable beach quality
What I Liked
  • Beach is genuinely uncrowded — 3 guests max during my stay
  • Tree pod dining is a once-in-a-lifetime experience
  • Snorkeling from shore rivals Maldives quality
  • 50% cheaper than comparable Maldives resort
What Could Be Better
  • Remote access: 1-hour flight + 45-min speedboat
  • $1,200/night is expensive by Thailand standards
  • No nightlife or nearby restaurants — you're committed to the resort

2. Six Senses Samui

Verified Stay

Six Senses Samui occupies a private peninsula on Koh Samui's north coast. I stayed in a Hillside Villa (130 sqm) with a private infinity pool overlooking the Gulf of Thailand. The villa is designed for privacy — thick tropical vegetation screens each unit from neighbors. The beach is smaller than Soneva Kiri's but equally beautiful, with calm water perfect for swimming.

At $600/night, it's half the price of Soneva Kiri while delivering excellent beach and spa experiences. The spa's "Sleeping Pod" treatment (90 minutes, $150) involved a heated stone bed overlooking the ocean — deeply relaxing. The food at Dining on the Rocks is the best I had in Koh Samui. The resort's sustainability program includes organic gardens, solar panels, and a recycling center. Koh Samui has better infrastructure than Koh Kood — restaurants, nightlife, and day trips are easily accessible.

Key Details

  • Location: North coast of Koh Samui
  • Price: $400-1,000 per night
  • Villa: Hillside Villa, 130 sqm, private infinity pool
  • Beach: Small but beautiful, calm swimming water
  • Spa: "Sleeping Pod" treatment with ocean views
  • Sustainability: Organic gardens, solar panels, recycling
What I Liked
  • Private peninsula location — genuine seclusion
  • Spa Sleeping Pod treatment is unique and relaxing
  • Half the price of Soneva with excellent quality
  • Koh Samui infrastructure — restaurants and nightlife accessible
What Could Be Better
  • Beach is smaller than Soneva Kiri
  • Hillside villas require a steep walk or buggy ride to beach
  • Koh Samui is more developed — less "untouched island" feel

Phuket vs. Koh Samui vs. Koh Kood

  • Phuket: Largest island, most infrastructure, busiest beaches. Best for nightlife and dining. Crowded in peak season.
  • Koh Samui: Mid-sized island, good balance of development and nature. Best for families and couples. Calm beaches.
  • Koh Kood: Least developed, most pristine. Best for seclusion and nature. Limited infrastructure.

Final Thoughts

Soneva Kiri on Koh Kood offers the most pristine beach experience — uncrowded, excellent snorkeling, and tree pod dining. Six Senses Samui delivers 80% of the experience at 50% of the price with better infrastructure access. For beach purists, Koh Kood wins. For convenience and value, Koh Samui is the answer. Thailand's beach resorts genuinely rival the Maldives at a fraction of the cost.
