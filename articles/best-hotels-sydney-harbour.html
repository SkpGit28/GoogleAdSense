Best Hotels in Sydney with Harbour View

Jun 10, 2026 By Mr. Lee 8 min read
Advertisement

Sydney Harbour is one of the most beautiful natural harbours in the world, and staying at a hotel with harbour views puts you at the heart of Australia's most iconic scenery. From the Opera House to the Harbour Bridge, these views never get old.

In this guide, we've curated the best hotels in Sydney that offer stunning harbour views, from luxury properties in Circular Quay to boutique hotels in The Rocks.

What Makes a Great Harbour View?

The best harbour views in Sydney feature:

  • Sydney Opera House: The iconic sail-shaped building
  • Harbour Bridge: The famous "Coathanger" bridge
  • Water views: Direct sightlines to the harbour
  • City skyline: The downtown Sydney skyline as a backdrop
  • Harbour activities: Ferries, yachts, and sailing boats
Advertisement

Top Hotels with Harbour Views

1. Park Hyatt Sydney

Located directly across from the Opera House, Park Hyatt Sydney offers the most prestigious harbour views in the city. Every room features floor-to-ceiling windows with Opera House or Harbour Bridge views.

Why We Love It

  • Direct Opera House views from every room
  • Harbour Bridge views from select suites
  • Rooftop pool with panoramic views
  • Walking distance to Circular Quay
  • Exceptional dining with harbour views

2. InterContinental Sydney

Occupying a historic building in Circular Quay, InterContinental Sydney offers stunning harbour views from its elevated position. The hotel's rooftop bar provides 360-degree views of the harbour and city.

Why We Love It

  • Historic building with modern luxury
  • Multiple dining venues with harbour views
  • Rooftop bar with panoramic vistas
  • Central Circular Quay location
  • Walking distance to Opera House

3. Shangri-La Hotel Sydney

Rising 36 floors above The Rocks, Shangri-La offers sweeping harbour views from its elevated position. The hotel's Altitude restaurant provides fine dining with spectacular views.

4. Pullman Quay Grand Sydney Harbour

Located in East Circular Quay, these apartment-style accommodations offer spacious living with harbour views. The hotel's location provides easy access to ferries and harbour cruises.

5. The Sebel Quay West Suites Sydney

Offering apartment-style accommodation with harbour views, The Sebel provides excellent value for families and extended stays. The hotel's location near the Opera House makes it ideal for sightseeing.

Budget-Friendly Harbour View Options

  • Harbour Rocks Hotel: Boutique hotel in The Rocks with harbour glimpses
  • Pier One Sydney Harbour: Waterfront hotel with direct harbour access
  • Ovolo Woolloomooloo: Trendy hotel with harbour views from some rooms

Conclusion

Sydney's harbour view hotels offer an unparalleled experience, combining stunning scenery with world-class accommodations. Whether you choose a luxury property or a budget-friendly option, these hotels provide the perfect base for exploring Sydney.
