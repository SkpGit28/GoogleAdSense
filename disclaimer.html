The research basis of this site

Prime Hotel Picks is written by one person, Sushant Kumar, from published sources. That means hotel material, rates compared across booking platforms, guest reviews read in quantity, and maps.

I have not personally stayed at every hotel covered here, and nothing on this site should be read as a first-hand account of a stay unless it explicitly says so. Guides are research, and they are labelled as research. Where a fact could not be confirmed, the guide says that rather than guessing.

Rates and prices

Every rate quoted is an indicative range in Indian rupees with the month it was checked stated alongside it. It is not a quote, not an offer, and not a guarantee of availability.

Indian hotel rates change constantly and can move sharply with season, festivals, weddings, conferences and demand on a given date. Taxes, service charges, meal plans and transfers may or may not be included depending on the property and the rate you book. Always confirm the current total with the hotel or the booking platform before committing.

If you read a guide long after its stated check date, treat the numbers as historical context rather than current pricing.

No ratings or review counts are invented

This site does not publish star ratings, scores or aggregate review counts of its own for third-party hotels. Where guest sentiment is described, it reflects patterns observed across published reviews and is a summary, not a measurement.
